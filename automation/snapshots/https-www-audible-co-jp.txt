"Audible helps me get through my day whether I am working, showering, doing chores, shopping, exercising or just chilling." Frequently asked questions How does the free trial work? Eligible new members can sign up for a free trial anytime. During your free trial, you can enjoy the same benefits as a paying member. When your trial is over, you’ll be automatically charged a monthly subscription fee. You can cancel anytime. What do I get with a monthly Audible membership? As a Standard member, you can select 1 audiobook a month from our entire collection. You can listen to your selected audiobooks for as long as you’re a member, and purchase additional audiobooks for the full price. As a Premium member, you’ll get 1 credit a month to buy any title you like from our entire collection. This title is yours to keep, even if you cancel your membership. Credits rollover month to month until they expire or membership is cancelled. You’ll also get access to thousands of included titles in the all-you-can-listen catalogue and you can access Premium member-only sales and exclusive discounts on additional audiobook purchases. How much does Audible cost? Audible Standard is $8.99 per month Audible Premium Plus is $14.95 per month Each plan offers a 30-day free trial to new members. When your trial is over, you’ll be automatically charged a monthly subscription fee. Visit our Plans & pricing page to learn more. Do I have to commit for any period of time? There are no commitments. You can easily cancel your membership at anytime. All titles purchased with a credit are yours to keep forever. © Copyright 1997 - 2026 Audible Inc.
